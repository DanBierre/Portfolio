## Challenge

I'm learning Japanese and kept juggling three tools at once: a video player, a dictionary and Anki. I wanted one place where I could read or watch real Japanese, tap a word I don't know, and have it turn up for review later.

## What it does

The **Read** tab takes any Japanese you paste in and adds furigana over the kanji. Tap a word and a card shows its reading and meanings, and you can save it with one tap.

The **Watch** tab plays your own videos with their subtitle files. The current line shows with furigana and tappable words, and you can replay a line, slow things down or fix subtitles that are out of sync.

Saved words come back in **Review** as flashcards, spaced out so they actually stick. If you already use Anki, they can go straight there instead.

## Where it's at

I built it in 2025 and kept it to myself. Now I'm getting it ready to go public at mimitori.com.

It'll be free and open source (MIT), with no account and nothing uploaded: your files stay on your computer.
