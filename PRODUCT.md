# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Owners of small Auckland businesses: cafés, salons, trades and shops. Most aren't technical. They have a website that's broken, slow or looking tired, or no website at all. They want someone local they can trust who'll fix it at a fair price and talk to them in plain words.

## Product Purpose
Dan Bierre's personal site. It turns local visitors into enquiries through the brief form or email. Success is a steady trickle of Auckland jobs, not traffic.

## Positioning
"I build websites with an editor's eye." One local person with a film and 3D background who builds and fixes websites. Flat prices agreed before he starts, no jargon, and he'll come to you anywhere in Auckland. He'll also tell you when you don't need what you were about to buy.

## Operating Context
Owners look on their phone between customers, often from a link a mate sent. They judge whether Dan seems easy to deal with, check the price, and want a quick, low-risk way to ask a question.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Offer: Light Fix, $150 flat per fix (one problem, fixed and tested). Light Page, $450 flat (a clean one-page site). Bigger jobs get a written quote first.
- Contact: the brief form (sent by the Worker in `worker/`) or email.
- Domain: danbierre.dev. Hosted on Cloudflare Workers.
- English and Japanese (`/ja`). Japanese copy is drafted by Claude; Dan reviews it.
- Open: real client sites for the Client websites section (3 open spots for now).

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: relaxed Kiwi, warm and plain. Short sentences, contractions, "you" and "I". Understate things. NZ English spelling, prices in NZD.
- Banned words: revolutionary, cutting-edge, leverage, synergy, seamless, unlock, elevate, game-changer, supercharge.
- Trust lines: "Reply within 1 working day." · "I'll come to you, anywhere in Auckland." · "The price won't change."
- No pop-ups, chatbots, exit-intent modals, newsletter sign-up.

## Evidence on Hand
Mimitori (Dan's Japanese listening app), Shoji (his Windows planner), the Japan reel, and a before/after of his own site redesign. No client testimonials, logos or metrics yet; never fabricate them.

## Product Principles
1. Show, then tell: the site itself is the first sample of the work.
2. Prices up front, in plain words. No "contact us for pricing" for the small stuff.
3. A contact path is never more than one screen away.
4. Fast, accessible, and works without JavaScript, on a cheap phone on mobile data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA; full keyboard use; reduced-motion respected (the film effects stop).

## Visual Commitment
16mm film (2026-10-16), built on the earlier "Gallery Index" and shoji world (2026-10-15). An orange-graded Japan reel plays behind the hero, with moderate grain, film-burn cuts and scene slates per section, and the footer closes like end credits. Dan rejected text shake, kinetic type, iris wipes, whip cuts, 4:3 and heavy grain. The site is dark for every visitor. See DESIGN.md for the system.
