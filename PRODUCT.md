# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Creative agencies and studios (creative directors, producers, heads of innovation) who have won or are pitching AI-led briefs but lack the people to deliver them. They evaluate quickly, compare against other freelancers and dev shops, and fear delivery risk.

## Product Purpose
Dan Bierre's portfolio site. It turns agency visitors into qualified enquiries: booked 20-minute calls and submitted briefs. Success is enquiries from agencies, not traffic.

## Positioning
"The AI half of your creative team." One person who designs and builds both the client-facing AI experience and the generative pipeline behind it. Unlike a dev shop, thinks like a creative; unlike a creative, ships working systems.

## Operating Context
Agency buyers skim on desktop between meetings and on phones from links shared in Slack/LinkedIn. They judge craft from the site itself, then look for proof (case studies), process (how we work), and a low-risk way to start (call or brief).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Offer: Experiences (interactive AI campaigns, installations, AI features in client sites/apps) and Pipelines (custom generative image/video/copy workflows for the agency's own production).
- Contact: book a 20-min call (Cal.com), send a 3-question brief, email.
- Domain: danbierre.dev.
- Open: final case study content, Lab experiments, hero footage, client logos.

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: confident, plain English, agency-to-partner ("your client", "your team", "your pitch").
- Banned words: revolutionary, cutting-edge, leverage, synergy.
- Trust lines: "Reply within 1 working day." · "Happy to work white-label and sign your NDA."
- No pop-ups, chatbots, exit-intent modals, newsletter sign-up.

## Evidence on Hand
1–2 deep client case studies (content not yet supplied). No testimonials, logos, or metrics supplied yet; never fabricate them. Placeholders must be marked `[PLACEHOLDER]`.

## Product Principles
1. Depth over volume: few projects, told well.
2. Every section proves taste, reliability, or ease of working together.
3. A contact path is never more than one screen away.
4. The site itself is proof of engineering craft: fast, accessible, works without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark themes; full keyboard use; reduced-motion respected.

## Visual Commitment
Redesign (2026-10-15): Dan replaced the "classic studio" canon, judged too generic, with a signature world drawn from his references: Kolkhoze (Behance, Denys Brustovskyi) as the lead, and the Vècna architecture studio shot (Dribbble, Awsmd) as secondary. Dan chose "Gallery Index": near-black ground with flat light-grey bands, one Swiss neo-grotesk at extreme scale contrast, [bracket] counts, a lowercase index nav, and monochrome work that turns to colour on hover. Both signature moments are required: a diffusion reveal on the hero headline, and a pipeline reveal (how each piece was made) on the work index. Dan also asked for shoji (Japanese sliding paper screens) as a third influence: lattice grid, backlit washi bands, sliding-panel reveals. The site is dark for every visitor rather than following the device theme. Earlier craft bar: Pentagram, Locomotive, Studio Freight, Linear.
